# Classical texts and further learning

## Traditional texts referenced by this skill's interpretive framework

1. **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra** - the foundational text of Vedic
   astrology, attributed to Sage Parashara; source of the house-signification
   and planetary-nature framework used throughout this skill.
2. **Brihat Jataka** (Varahamihira) - a classical text on natal astrology,
   particularly influential for yoga definitions.
3. **Saravali** (Kalyana Varma) - a comprehensive horoscopy text covering
   yogas and planetary combinations in detail.
4. **Phaladeepika** (Mantreswara) - focused on predicting results from
   planetary positions and dasha periods.
5. **Jataka Parijata** (Vaidyanatha) - a natal astrology compendium, source
   for several dignity and strength rules.
6. **KP Readers** (K.S. Krishnamurti) - the foundational texts of the
   Krishnamurthi Paddhati system this skill's KP mode is based on.

## Ethical guidelines for interpretation

1. **Emphasize free will** - a chart shows tendencies and potentials, not
   fixed, unchangeable certainties.
2. **Be compassionate** - avoid fatalistic or alarming language, especially
   around health, death, or relationship endings.
3. **Offer constructive framing** - where classical remedies are relevant
   (mantra, charity, lifestyle adjustment), mention them as options, not
   mandates, and avoid suggesting specific expensive purchases (gemstones
   etc.) as a required fix.
4. **Respect privacy** - birth data and chart interpretations are personal;
   handle generated chart files accordingly (they're gitignored by default
   in this project for exactly this reason).
5. **Acknowledge uncertainty** - birth time accuracy, which library/ayanamsa
   was used, and the limitations documented in `known-limitations.md` all
   affect how precise a reading can be. State material uncertainty rather
   than presenting a computed chart as infallible.
6. **Know the limits of an automated reading** - for major life decisions,
   recommend consulting an experienced astrologer who can account for
   nuance this skill's deterministic scripts don't capture (see
   `yogas.md` on why yoga detection is left to the interpreting agent, not
   an automated engine).
