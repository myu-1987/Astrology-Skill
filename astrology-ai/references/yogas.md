# Yogas (planetary combinations)

`interpret_chart.py` does not auto-detect these (see its module docstring
for why) - use this reference plus a chart's raw `d1_chart`/`divisional_charts`
data to reason about whether a combination applies.

## Supportive yogas

- **Raja Yoga**: formed when lords of Kendra houses (1/4/7/10) and Trikona
  houses (1/5/9) are connected - by conjunction, mutual aspect, or exchange
  of signs (Parivartana). Indicates power, status, and authority.
- **Dhana Yoga**: wealth-giving combinations, typically formed when lords of
  houses 2, 5, 9, or 11 are connected to each other or to the 1st lord.
- **Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga**: a debilitated planet's weakness is cancelled
  (see `planetary-dignities.md`) and the planet instead gives unusually
  strong, sometimes spectacular, results - especially rise after early
  struggle.
- **Gajakesari Yoga**: Jupiter is in a Kendra (1/4/7/10) from the Moon.
  Gives wisdom, respect, and steady prosperity; strength varies with
  Jupiter's own dignity in that placement.
- **Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas**: five yogas formed when Mars, Mercury,
  Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn occupies a Kendra house in its own sign or
  exaltation sign - respectively named Ruchaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya, and
  Shasha Yoga. Each confers strong results tied to that planet's
  significations.

## Challenging yogas

- **Kemadruma Yoga**: the Moon has no planets (other than Rahu/Ketu) in the
  houses immediately before or after it (2nd/12th from Moon), and no
  planets conjunct it either. Associated with emotional isolation or
  financial struggle, though considered weakened or cancelled by various
  classical exceptions (e.g. the Moon in a Kendra from Lagna, or aspected by
  a benefic).
- **Daridra Yoga**: poverty-indicating combinations, typically when the 11th
  lord (house of gains) is poorly placed in a dusthana (6/8/12) or
  conjunct/aspected by malefics without redeeming factors.
- **Kala Sarpa Yoga**: all seven classical planets (Sun through Saturn) fall
  on one side of the Rahu-Ketu axis, with no planet crossing it. Considered
  to create a single-minded, often obstacle-laden life path with delayed
  but sometimes dramatic results; its severity and variants (named by which
  house Rahu occupies) are debated across classical and modern sources.

These definitions are a starting reference, not an exhaustive catalog - the
classical texts in `classical-texts.md` describe dozens more, many specific
to particular house/planet combinations.
