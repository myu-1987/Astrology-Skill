# House significations (Parashara system)

| House | Sanskrit | Significations |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lagna/Tanu | Self, body, personality, overall vitality and general fortune |
| 2 | Dhana | Wealth, family, speech, food, accumulated resources, early education |
| 3 | Sahaja | Siblings, courage, communication, short journeys, effort, skills |
| 4 | Sukha | Mother, home, property, vehicles, emotional comfort, education (primary) |
| 5 | Putra | Children, intelligence, creativity, romance, speculation, past-life merit |
| 6 | Ripu | Enemies, disease, debt, service, obstacles, litigation, daily work |
| 7 | Kalatra | Spouse, marriage, business partnerships, public dealings |
| 8 | Ayu/Randhra | Longevity, inheritance, occult matters, sudden events, transformation |
| 9 | Dharma | Father, higher education, fortune, long journeys, guru, religion/philosophy |
| 10 | Karma | Career, profession, public status, authority, fame, actions in the world |
| 11 | Labha | Gains, income, friendships, ambitions, elder siblings, fulfillment of desires |
| 12 | Vyaya | Losses, expenses, foreign residence, spirituality, isolation, liberation |

## Groupings commonly used in interpretation

- **Kendra (angular)**: 1, 4, 7, 10 — houses of action and foundation; strongest houses structurally.
- **Trikona (trine)**: 1, 5, 9 — houses of fortune and dharma; benefic by nature.
- **Dusthana (malefic houses)**: 6, 8, 12 — houses of difficulty, debt, loss, and transformation.
- **Upachaya (growth houses)**: 3, 6, 10, 11 — houses that improve with malefic influence/effort over time.
- **Maraka (death-inflicting) houses**: 2 and 7 — their lords are considered capable of triggering endings, not literal death predictors in isolation.

These groupings explain *why* certain houses are combined when judging a
life area (see `kp-judgment-rules.md` for the specific house groups this
skill's `interpret_chart.py` uses per focus area).
