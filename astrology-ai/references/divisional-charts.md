The classical "Shodashvarga" (16-varga) set is D1, D2, D3, D4, D7, D9, D10,
D12, D16, D20, D24, D27, D30, D40, D45, D60 — this skill's engine
(`jyotishganit`) computes all of these except D6, D8, and D11.

## How to use divisional charts

A standard approach: read D1 for the overall picture, then confirm or refine
a specific life area with its dedicated varga - D9 for marriage, D10 for
career, D7 for children, D4 for property. A planet or house lord that is
weak in D1 but strong in the relevant divisional chart often still delivers
reasonable results in that specific area, and vice versa. Birth time
accuracy matters more for divisional charts than for D1, since their house
divisions are finer-grained (e.g. D60 divides each sign into 60 slices of
0.5 degrees) - a few minutes of birth-time error can shift a D60 placement
noticeably more than a D1 one.
